English

This policy describes how Panda Link Selector, developed by Marcos Yang, handles information when you select and open webpage links using a rectangle or lasso.

Information processed on your device

The extension reads webpage links and their displayed positions to determine which links intersect or fall inside your selection. The selection path, selected URLs, and opening results are processed in browser memory. They are used to open the links you select and display the result.

The extension stores its activation state and selection rule in the browser's local extension storage. These settings apply across tabs and windows in the same browser profile. The extension's own storage does not synchronize these settings to a developer-operated server.

Transmission and third parties

The developer receives no browsing history, webpage content, selected URLs, or usage analytics from the extension. The extension contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking service and does not sell or share browsing data with third parties.

Opening selected links causes your browser to connect to the destination websites through normal navigation. Those websites and the browser handle their own network requests, history, cookies, and data practices. Those activities are governed by their respective policies.

Permissions

Website access enables the floating control and link selection on supported pages. The scripting permission restores the selector on existing supported tabs when needed. The storage permission saves your activation state and selection rule. The extension uses browser tab APIs to open the selected links in the source window.

Retention and control

Selection data is held in memory during use and while requested tabs are being opened. Local settings remain until removed through the browser's extension-data controls or by uninstalling the extension. The browser separately manages tabs and browsing history. You can turn off selection with Esc or the popup switch and manage website access in the browser's extension settings.

Contact and updates

For questions, contact Marcos Yang at marcosyang183@gmail.com. If you email the developer, your message and email address are handled for responding to your request through the email provider. Policy changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

简体中文

本政策说明 Marcos Yang 开发的熊猫链接选择器（Panda Link Selector） 在用户通过矩形或套索选择并打开网页链接时如何处理信息。

设备内处理的信息

插件读取网页链接及其显示位置，判断链接是否与选区接触或位于选区内。选区轨迹、选中链接地址和打开结果在浏览器内存中处理，用于打开用户选中的链接及显示操作结果。

插件在浏览器的本地扩展存储中保存激活状态和选中规则。这些设置用于同一浏览器配置中的标签页与窗口同步，插件自身不会将其同步到开发者运营的服务器。

数据传输与第三方

开发者不会通过插件接收浏览历史、网页内容、选中链接地址或使用统计。插件未接入统计分析、广告或跟踪服务，也不出售或向第三方共享浏览数据。

打开选中链接时，浏览器会正常访问目标网站。目标网站和浏览器按照各自的政策处理网络请求、浏览记录、Cookie 等信息。

权限用途

网站访问权限用于在支持的页面上显示悬浮控件和选择链接。脚本权限用于按需在已打开且受支持的标签页中恢复选择器。存储权限用于保存激活状态和选中规则。插件通过浏览器标签页接口在来源窗口打开用户选中的链接。

保存期限与用户控制

选择数据在使用期间及批量打开标签页的任务期间保存在内存中。本地设置保留至用户通过浏览器扩展数据管理功能删除或卸载插件。浏览器单独管理标签页和浏览历史。用户可以通过 Esc 或面板开关退出选择，并在浏览器扩展设置中管理网站访问权限。
